The challenge

Katcon's Plant 2 runs 25 production lines and 300+ machines. Failure records were logged inconsistently by interns, with no common structure, so downtime couldn't be traced to a failure mode or to the area responsible. KPI reports were built by hand, no dashboard showed the targets, and the plant needed a reliable baseline for its internal and customer audits under IATF 16949. My task was to turn all of that into a single source of truth for maintenance supervisors, from the whole plant down to a single machine.

What I did

  • Redesigned the failure reporting system: restructured the fields of the failure log and standardized a classification by failure mode, root component and responsible area across 400+ records.
  • Set up the MTTR, MTBF and downtime reporting framework and calculated the reliability baseline from 2025 data.
  • Built an end-to-end Power BI platform for Plant 2 on top of it, refreshing automatically from Excel files on SharePoint, with weekly, monthly and annual reports, drill-down by area, line, shift, technician and system, targets visible on every chart, and a failure explorer that links each event to its root cause. I learned DAX and Power Query from scratch to build it.
  • Adapted the same system for Plant 1, to its own lines, equipment and needs.

Results

  • MTTR went from a 75-minute average in 2025 to 53 minutes in 2026 year-to-date by the end of my internship (−29%), below the plant's 60-minute target, through action plans the framework helped set and track.
  • Maintenance engineers adopted the new failure log directly, replacing the inconsistent intern-led logging.
  • The reliability baseline supported the plant's internal and customer IATF 16949 audits.
  • The platform is still in use by the maintenance team after my internship ended.
  • Before leaving, I trained colleagues to run and maintain the reports.

Also during the internship

  • Standardized autonomous maintenance (TPM) procedures, with visual work instructions and inspection checklists, for 30+ machines and 8 new production lines during ramp-up.
  • Built a spare parts catalog in Power BI with 1,000+ parts, searchable by SAP number, part number, brand, category and line, with minimum and maximum stock levels, and wrote its user manual.
