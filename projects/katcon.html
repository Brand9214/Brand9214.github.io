Operations intelligence platform

A Power BI platform that shows failures and downtime for 25 production lines and 300+ machines, from the whole plant down to a single technician or system, built on a failure classification I standardized during my internship.

Company
Katcon, automotive supplier in Monterrey
My role
Maintenance intern
Dates
Sep 2025 – Jun 2026
Users
Maintenance supervisors

What I did

  • Built an end-to-end Power BI operations intelligence platform covering 25 production lines and 300+ machines, with drill-down by area, line, shift, technician and system, and an interactive failure analysis tool that links each event to its root cause.
  • Led the redesign of the plant failure reporting system, standardizing a failure classification by failure mode, affected component and responsible area, so downtime can be analyzed and attributed across functions.
  • Established the MTTR, MTBF and downtime reporting framework and calculated the reliability baseline, enabling line-level targets and supporting action plans that moved MTTR from a 75-minute baseline to 53 minutes.
  • Standardized autonomous maintenance (TPM) procedures, with visual work instructions and inspection checklists, across 30+ machines and 8 new production lines during ramp-up.
