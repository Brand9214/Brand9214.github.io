Automated robotic insert loading cell

Inserts were loaded by hand into an automotive glass molding press, in a 120-second manual cycle. Our team took the operation from a plant floor assessment to a robotic cell design and a working prototype that runs the full 12-insert sequence with the operator out of the press area.

What I did

  • Led the project from the plant floor assessment to the robotic cell, coordinating the mechanical, controls, vision and economic workstreams.
  • Designed the cell around an ABB IRB 1300 with PLC control and a custom three-finger gripper that handles four insert geometries without a tool change, with Cognex vision verifying every pick before the robot commits to it. In simulation, the cell cuts the 120-second manual cycle to under 50 seconds.
  • Built the system as a working prototype on a UR3 collaborative robot, running the complete loading sequence in under 80 seconds with the operator fully out of the press area.

Equipment and tools

  • ABB IRB 1300
  • Universal Robots UR3
  • PLC control
  • Cognex vision
  • Custom three-finger gripper
