Automated robotic insert loading cell

The challenge

Inserts were loaded into the press by hand, in a 120-second manual cycle for the 12-insert sequence. The goal was to automate the loading and take the operator out of the press area.

What I did

  • Led the project from the plant floor assessment to the robotic cell, coordinating the mechanical, controls, vision and economic workstreams.
  • Designed the cell around an ABB IRB 1300 with PLC control and a custom three-finger gripper that handles four insert geometries without a tool change, with Cognex vision verifying every pick before the robot commits to it.
  • Built the system as a working prototype on a UR3 collaborative robot, running the complete loading sequence in under 80 seconds with the operator fully out of the press area.
