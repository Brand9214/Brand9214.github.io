About

Most recently, I worked at Katcon México, where I developed a Power BI maintenance analytics platform covering production lines and equipment across the plant. I also worked on failure analysis, process standardization, and TPM autonomous maintenance standards during the expansion and launch of new production lines.

Throughout my degree, I collaborated with industry partners on engineering projects combining automation, simulation, robotics, and manufacturing. These included ABB, where I designed and simulated a robotic automation cell in RobotStudio; Carrier, where I worked with digital twins using Siemens Tecnomatix Plant Simulation and Process Simulate; and Vitro, where I led a robotic press automation project that earned 3rd place at ExpoIngeniería. I also participated in an autonomous vehicle project with John Deere.

My academic background includes a concentration in Space Technologies, with coursework in embedded systems, wireless communications, RF systems, and orbital mechanics.
